Unofficial view of SoundComparisons.com
About

This is not the Sound Comparisons website

This page is an unofficial, mobile-friendly interface to SoundComparisons.com. It only rearranges the original site's content for small screens. It is not made, endorsed or maintained by the Sound Comparisons team.

All recordings, phonetic transcriptions, word lists, language varieties, regions and map locations come from Sound Comparisons. Recordings play directly from their server, and the text data is a copy of what their public site serves, refreshed weekly.

Original authors

Sound Comparisons was founded by Paul Heggarty, with many contributors who recorded, transcribed and edited the material. See the full list of contributors on the original website.
